Habits to take from it

    Which contracts win the auction

    Every contract that won an auction, by level and attachment: its share of the deals, how often it was made, and the declarer's average score from it (tripled when the declarer played alone). Darker cells win more auctions.

    What one more card is worth

    The change from one more of each in the original 13 cards, the rest of the hand the same: least-squares fits over every seat. Whiskers are 95% intervals.

    Points per deal

    Chance to win the auction (percentage points)

    Hand strength, bidding and winning

    Among seats with each count: how many bid at all, how many won the auction, and how many of those made their contract. Hover for the seats' average score.

    One long suit or two

    Points per deal a hand's shape adds beyond its high cards: the seats' average score less what their Jokers, aces, kings, queens and jacks predict. Rows are the longest suit, columns the second longest.
