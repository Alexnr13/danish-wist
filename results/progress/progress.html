Danish Wist: training progress

The workstation line of the learned bot: rl-005 ran 4000 iterations from rl-004d's 10, and rl-006 carried on from rl-005's iteration 3600, for 18,000 iterations; from its 4000 (7600 here) its league kept only the newest 20 exploiters, and from its 6000 (9600 here) it trained one every 200 iterations instead of 100 and kept its magnet, its weights averaged over about the last 100 iterations, which is what is chosen from then on. rl-007 carried rl-006 on from its 18,000 (21,600 here) with its update graphed, the same learning, and was stopped at its 23,713 (27,313 here) by a stop rule: two of its league's exploiters in a row found a significant margin. One iteration is 1024 deals of self-play. Points are per deal, against RuleBot unless said otherwise; bars and bands are 95% intervals.
